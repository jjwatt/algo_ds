*** 2-50

*** 2-54

money. Then the pirates vote. If the senior pirate gets at least half
the votes he wins, and that division remains. If he doesn't, he is
killed and then the next senior-most pirate gets a chance to propose
the division. Now tell what will happen and why (i.e. how many pirates
survive and how the division is done)? All the pirates are intelligent
and the first priority is to stay alive and the next priority is to
get as much money as possible.
